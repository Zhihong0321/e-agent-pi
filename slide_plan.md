## Slide 12 — What's Not Solved Yet

**Goal:** Show you've thought past the pitch into the hard part — this is what makes Management trust the ask on Slide 14.

**Headline:** "What has to be true before we can sell this"

**Content:**
- **Multi-tenancy:** in-house = one company, one setup. Selling means per-customer data isolation, billing, admin roles — real engineering work, not a toggle
- **WhatsApp Business API costs:** Meta charges per conversation at scale; costs that were negligible in-house may look different multiplied across many paying customers — needs modeling before pricing is locked
- **Go-to-market motion:** SME sales in Malaysia is a distribution problem (channel partners, resellers, maybe telco/accounting-software bundling) as much as a product problem — this is a different muscle than building the product
- **Support model:** SMEs will need help, not documentation — who owns onboarding and support once this isn't just internal users?

**Visual:** Checklist-style, 4 items, framed as "before we scale" not "reasons to worry."
