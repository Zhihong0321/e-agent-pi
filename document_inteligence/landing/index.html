What it handles

Five workflows, one database.

Each workflow has its own assistant, and every assistant uses the same customers, products and company rules. Nothing is copied from one place to another by hand.

Customers and contacts

Paste a name card or lead form. Existing customers are found before a duplicate is made.

  • Matches SSM, TIN, email, phone and name
  • Fills blank fields only, never overwrites

Quotations, invoices and payments

Draft from plain words, check the readiness rules, issue, convert and record payment.

  • Asks every missing question in one message
  • PDF generated when a document is issued
  • Unpaid invoices can be voided; paid ones cannot

Public forms

Design a form from a fixed set of field types and publish a link. Answers become customers or documents only when you say so.

  • Server-side validation on every submission
  • Passwords, card numbers and OTPs are never collected

Expense claims

Staff send a receipt in chat. Each receipt becomes a claim, grouped into the monthly submission by the company's cut-off day.

  • Admins approve, reject or close the month
  • Closed months are frozen and get a report PDF

Procurement

Suppliers, their quotations and invoices, and purchase orders from draft to received.

  • Invoice checked against its purchase order
  • Only an admin issues, cancels or marks paid
How it works

From a sentence to an issued quotation.

The assistant thinks before it writes. It looks up the customer and the catalogue, tests the company's rules against a draft, and only then asks you for what is missing.

  1. You describe it in plain words

    "Quotation for Acme, the 10 kWp package and installation." No forms to fill in first.

  2. It checks before anything is saved

    It finds the customer by SSM, TIN, email, phone or name, looks up the products, and runs the readiness rules on a draft. Nothing is written yet.

  3. It asks everything at once

    One message with every open question and a sensible suggestion, such as a valid-until date 30 days from today.

  4. You issue it

    The number is assigned now, inside the same transaction, so it cannot leave a gap. The PDF is rendered, and the customer and company details are frozen on the document.

  5. Later steps are logged too

    An accepted quotation converts to an invoice. Payments are recorded against it. Each change is attributed to a person or an assistant.

Safety

The rules live in the database, not in the prompt.

A prompt can be talked around. Database grants, triggers and row-level security cannot. These are the guarantees, and each one is enforced where the data is stored.

Deletion

Nothing is hard-deleted

Removing a record is a soft delete you can restore. The database refuses DELETE and TRUNCATE for every role, including the owner.

Issued documents

Issued documents are frozen

After issue, the content cannot change. Only status, payment, void and PDF fields can still move. Line items are locked.

Numbering

Numbers have no gaps

A number is assigned only when a document is issued, inside the same transaction, with a row lock on the sequence.

Isolation

Companies cannot see each other

Every table has a row-level security policy on its tenant. The check runs on writes as well as reads.

Audit

Every change is attributed

Before and after values, who acted and which assistant acted are written to a log. The application role cannot edit or delete that log.

Assistants

Assistants cannot change the structure

No schema changes, no shell, no SQL, no file editing. Each assistant sees only its own tools, and no assistant holds database credentials.

Custom fields, readiness rules and tax codes are data. A company can change how its documents work without anyone editing the code.

Built around Malaysian paperwork

SST, SSM and phone numbers, handled the Malaysian way.

The matching and the tax setup are written for how Malaysian companies record their details, not for a generic template.

SST tax codes

Each company gets its own tax codes. The default is NT, with the others ready to use.

NTSV8SV6ST10ST5EX

Duplicate matching

Phone numbers are normalised, so 012-345 6789 and +60 12-345 6789 are recognised as the same number.

Company names

"Sdn Bhd", "Enterprise" and similar words are ignored when names are compared. Free email domains are never treated as a company's domain.

MyInvois-ready fields Submission later

TIN, MSIC, SST number and e-invoice status are stored, and missing TINs are flagged. Sending to LHDN is not connected yet.

Status

What works today, and what does not yet.

This is a working MVP run by one operator. We would rather you know the limits now than find them after you have relied on the tool.

Working today

Built, with tests in the repository.

  • Customers, products, packages and catalogue lookup
  • Quotations, invoices, issue, conversion, payments and void
  • Public forms with submissions, review and CSV export
  • Expense claims, monthly submissions and claim reports
  • Suppliers, purchase orders, goods received and the invoice-to-PO check
  • Company setup, reset with a snapshot, and the safety guarantees above

Not yet

On the list, not built. No dates promised here.

  • Named user accounts and rolesToday the app runs as a single operator.
  • Sending documents by emailDocuments are rendered as PDFs. They are not sent for you.
  • MyInvois submission to LHDNOnly the fields are ready.
  • Credit notes as a full workflowThe type and numbering exist. The workflow does not.
  • Business reports such as ageing and sales by customer or product
  • Partial payments on purchase orders, and foreign-currency documents
  • Second-check review in the live chatBuilt and tested in the stress harness. Not yet in the live chat.
Getting started

Three steps before the first document.

Documents cannot be issued until the company details are complete. The readiness rules say what is still missing, so nothing has to be guessed.

1

Set up the company

Name, country, business type and activity, currency, and an email or phone. Add the legal name, SSM, address and bank details before you issue.

2

Add products or packages

Name, price and SST code for each item. Changes to a product never alter a document that was already issued.

3

Describe your first quotation

Say it in plain words, answer the questions, then issue. The PDF link appears in the chat.
