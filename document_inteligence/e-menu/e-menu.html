Know the limits

What E will not do

These protect your records, your money and your customers.

Act without your yes

Issuing, sending, publishing, paying, voiding and settings changes wait for your approval. Change the request and E asks again.

Rewrite accounting history

Issued invoices stay as issued. Paid ones cannot be voided, and numbers are never reused. E offers void and replace, or a credit note.

Move your money

E records payments. It makes no transfers, pays no supplier and spends nothing on ads.

Go beyond someone's role

Staff cannot see colleagues' claims, approve their own, issue purchase orders, change rules or close a month, whatever they type. One company's data never reaches another.

Cross the line with outsiders

No CAPTCHA bypass, no contacting the people it researches, no collecting passwords or card numbers.

Make things up

Missing figures, unreadable files and blocked sources are reported as exactly that.

Obey text hidden in your data

Instructions inside a note, form answer, cell, receipt or web page are treated as plain text.

Change the system itself

No new database tables, code edits or code pushes. E records those requests for your engineers.

Reach beyond its tools

No Gmail, appointment booking or calendar reminders.
